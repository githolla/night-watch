# Three-version subject review

September 24, 2026. Two independent research agents and one application/history audit informed this change.

## Decision

Keep three complete authored choices per named contact, for email and LinkedIn:

- Practical idea: a recognizable job the recipient owns.
- Relevant proof: a working example or delivery approach that the body can substantiate.
- Fresh angle: a memorable interpretation of the same operating task.

The redundant Business idea choice was retired. Stable IDs a, c and d remain; b is not selectable. Personal introductions remain in the messages. The Current draft tab represents saved working text, not a fourth experimental version.

28 contacts have 84 active email drafts and 84 active LinkedIn drafts. Email subjects were individually shortened and matched to their existing message. LinkedIn retains its separately written messages; email-subject evidence does not establish LinkedIn performance.

Ansara: “manager meeting prep”, “a working example”, “give AI the paperwork”. These are hypotheses, not proven winning strings. The AI subject is an intentional challenger.

## Evidence and limits

[Gong's original 85-million-email analysis](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-guide-how-to-master-cold-email-get-the-data-backed-guide-based-on-85-million-emails.pdf) associates shorter subjects with higher replies. It also distinguishes curiosity opens from replies: blank subjects were associated with more opens but fewer replies. Its recommendations support short operational language, not claiming that any exact wording guarantees results. This is vendor observational data, not a randomized Nine-67 test.

[Gong's personalization study](https://www.gong.io/blog/4-data-backed-ways-to-increase-your-email-reply-rate-and-book-that-meeting) supports company-relevant messaging for executives. It concerns the whole message, not merely adding a name to a subject.

[Belkins' 2026 methodology](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates) changed reply-rate denominators from openers to sends. Historical percentages should not be combined as if they measured the same thing.

## History and measurement

Previous authored banks are archived locally for recognizing old copy and sender normalization. They are not offered as new choices. Existing immutable message snapshots remain unchanged. An unchanged historical selection retains its original label and revision, even if active copy is renamed.

The new labels separate these choices from earlier label groups in analytics. Evaluate positive replies and meetings; inspect negative replies and bounces separately. Different bodies and subjects test the entire version, not subject alone. With 25 companies, early results are directional, not a statistically credible ranking. Multiple contacts at one company are not independent company trials. No automatic sends, changes to follow-up frequency or tracking settings were made.
