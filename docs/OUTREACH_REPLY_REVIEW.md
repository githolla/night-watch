# Outreach reply review, September 24, 2026

Three agents reviewed email, LinkedIn and the recipient's perspective. The integrating editor checked the revised copy, current-draft wiring, sender rendering and history preservation. All 252 active draft placements were revised: 112 email variants, 112 LinkedIn variants and 28 default contact emails across 25 companies.

## Changes that matter

- Replace first-contact project-scoping work with one easy question about a concrete proposal or relevant proof.
- Use the actual company situation and the recipient's role. Treat needs as hypotheses, not known operational failures.
- Distinguish Personal, Business idea, With proof and Wildcard by substance, not just tone.
- Explain Nine-67 to strangers: AI engineers who build with operating teams, not advice the recipient must implement alone.
- Use truthful proof without implying a professional-services result already exists in the recipient's sector.
- Remove duplicated questions, unsupported staffing/growth assumptions, unearned familiarity and malformed wording.

Saved email bodies are 60–83 words; LinkedIn messages are 39–57 words. All 224 variants contain one final question, retain their IDs and labels, and contain no em/en dashes. Greetings and sender names still follow settings. The review changes copy, not UI, addresses, account selection or sent history.

## Using the revisions

Refresh the reach-out list and choose the desired saved version. Edited, approved and sent copies remain preserved; choosing Use version deliberately applies the revised copy. Blank LinkedIn drafts start with the revised Personal version. Untouched email defaults use the existing refresh process.

No prospect messages were sent during this review. These revisions are editorial hypotheses, not proven reply improvements. Compare human and positive replies by channel and version. Keep the actual sent snapshots and revision IDs when assessing performance; historical sends are not retroactively rewritten. Open detections do not establish human attention. Revenue fit, contact deliverability and buying intent remain separate qualifications.
