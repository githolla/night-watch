# Second independent email review

Reviewed all 112 saved email drafts, including full bodies and subjects, for 28 contacts across 25 accounts. Changed 65 drafts: 61 bodies plus four additional subject-only changes. Preserved 47 drafts exactly. All four IDs, labels, contact identities and sender substitution remain unchanged.

## What the second pass found

The previous revision still relied too heavily on teaching experienced leaders obvious operating principles. Personal messages often repeated the same workflow in two paragraphs and ended by asking whether work was "still manual." Business idea versions mostly offered a sketch rather than useful engineering help. Those patterns could attract vague agreement without identifying real interest.

This pass rewrote the 28 Personal and 28 Business idea bodies. Personal now establishes company or public-statement context and offers one concrete way to help. Business idea starts with what Nine-67 would build, who uses it, and how the first version would be evaluated. The response is an indication of interest in that work, not an admission of poor operations or a request to scope our project.

Five Wildcard bodies received targeted changes. The others were retained. The Chrysan message no longer assumes the company has no existing AI applications. Native's indirect-question grammar was corrected. All 28 proof bodies were preserved: they offer actual case evidence or the owner-supplied deployment process as a lower-commitment way to evaluate fit.

Subjects were reviewed separately. Selected changes make the proposed outcome or evidence clearer rather than adding urgency or vague "AI opportunity" language.

## Representative changes

### Ansara, Personal

Before: "Ansara's location reports can tell you what happened. The more interesting question is whether the next review shows what got fixed." The question asked whether following up was still manual.

After: "For Ansara's restaurants across Michigan and northwest Ohio, I'd help managers turn a location review into the next action, with the supporting numbers ready." The message explains the engineer's deliverable, manager participation and evaluation, then asks whether the leader wants help turning one recurring review into a working tool.

Why: replaces a lesson and an inefficiency admission with a relevant offer.

### Gen Oil, Business idea

Before: "A completed delivery and an invoice-ready record are two different checkpoints." The close offered to sketch a delivery-ticket check.

After: "We'd build a ticket-to-order check for Gen Oil, so the office can review discrepancies before preparing an invoice." The closing question asks whether shortening the work between delivery and invoicing is a priority the leader wants help with.

Why: names the business result and the work Nine-67 would take on. It does not promise faster payment or a reduction in errors without evidence.

### Cayman / Kirk Maxey, Business idea

Before: an obvious standard about checking an answer, followed by permission to sketch it.

After: proposes testing strong evidence, incomplete evidence and unsupported questions. Sources remain visible and scientific review stays with the team. The CTA asks about help building an evidence-retrieval assistant held to that standard.

Why: turns attributed scientific-validation context into a specific acceptance test and an offer to deliver, rather than soliciting agreement with a good principle.

## Intentional tradeoff

Personal tests interest in a useful output or specific help, without requiring a project or supplier decision. Business idea makes the direct delivery offer and asks about a focused build. Proof and Wildcard retain lower-commitment evidence and capability tests. These intentionally different levels of commitment give the sender a meaningful choice. None should be called a proven winner before actual qualified replies are observed.

No message asks for confidential documents, an immediate meeting, or a detailed project specification. No guaranteed outcome, sector success, ROI, delivery date, urgency, or current buying intent was added. No revenue, company, person or address evidence was changed.
