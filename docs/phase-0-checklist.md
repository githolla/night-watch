# Phase 0 checklist

## Before night one

- [ ] Sync the maintained 100-company target universe from Settings.
- [ ] Complete `positioning.md` and approve the three proof points.
- [ ] Confirm Josh and Jenna's Gmail senders.
- [ ] Confirm Apollo access and use only verified results.
- [ ] Export team LinkedIn connections for manual path checks.
- [ ] Confirm nine-67.com SPF, DKIM, and enforced DMARC before the first email.
- [ ] Agree on the opt-out line appended to each email.

## Every night (five nights)

- [ ] Select 20 eligible accounts.
- [ ] Research changes from the prior 48 hours.
- [ ] Deduplicate normalized source URLs.
- [ ] Record signals with confidence of at least 0.6.
- [ ] Map each signal to the likely budget owner.
- [ ] Verify email in Apollo.
- [ ] Score the signal/person pair.
- [ ] Draft cards scoring at least 60.
- [ ] Review every send manually.
- [ ] Record the run, sends, replies, and cost.

## Exit review

- [ ] At least 10 cards were worth acting on.
- [ ] At least 2 replies were received.
- [ ] The team agrees signal quality justifies Phase 1.
- [ ] If not, document ICP or signal-type changes and repeat the proof.
